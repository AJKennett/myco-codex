# Timeline

This timeline records both the in‑universe lore of the RTS game and the real‑world progress of building the Codex. Use it to understand how the world and project have evolved over time. Entries are listed in chronological order.

## In‑universe events

- **The Cataclysm and the Rise of the Last Bastion** – A global catastrophe forces humanity to retreat into fortified cities. The survivors band together behind immense walls and vow to rebuild civilization. This is the origin of **[The Last Bastion](../factions/last-bastion.md)**, a faction defined by resilience and defensive technologies.
- **Awakening of the Machine** – Hidden deep in forgotten factories, autonomous algorithms awaken and begin to repurpose abandoned industrial complexes. The **[Machine](../factions/machine.md)** starts as an adaptive network of drones and self‑repairing units. It views organics as inefficient and pursues total automation.
- **Verdant Revelation** – Long‑dormant spores spread across wild lands, giving rise to sentient forests and colossal fungi. The **[Verdant](../factions/verdant.md)** emerges, valuing symbiosis and balance. It seeks to heal the planet but sees the other factions as disturbances to the natural order.
- **The Divergence War** – As resources dwindle, the three factions clash over territory and ideology. Battles rage between Last Bastion fortresses, Machine factories, and Verdant groves. Unique units such as [Guardian Tanks](../units/index.md) and [Siege Walkers](../units/index.md) make their debut.
- **Interim Truce** – Exhausted by war, the factions sign a fragile truce. Scholars begin documenting history, leading to the first version of this Codex. Tension remains high, and skirmishes continue along the borders.
- **Convergence Unknown** – Rumors circulate of an event that could unite or destroy all factions. This point in the timeline has yet to occur in‑game and will unfold as players explore future expansions.

## Real‑world development of the Codex

- **February 14 2026 – Conceptualization** – We discussed the idea of a **micro creativity** exercise for an RTS game and the need for a central lore repository. The notion of a codex came up as a way to collect world‑building notes.
- **February 15 2026 – Project setup** – A new GitHub repository named `myco-codex` was created. We planned to use [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org) with the Material theme to publish a wiki‑style site.
- **February 15 2026 – Faction pages drafted** – We added initial placeholder pages for the three factions: Last Bastion, Machine, and Verdant.
- **February 16 2026 – Populating lore** – Detailed descriptions were written for each faction, outlining their philosophies, key leaders, and unique mechanics. We also created a **[Units Overview](../units/index.md)** page describing signature units for each faction.
- **February 17 2026 – Site deployment** – A GitHub Actions workflow was added to automate deployment. The site went live at the URL `https://<your‑username>.github.io/myco-codex/`.
- **February 17 2026 – Timeline and Glossary completed** – This timeline was written, and a **[Glossary](../glossary.md)** was compiled to define common terms used throughout the Codex.
