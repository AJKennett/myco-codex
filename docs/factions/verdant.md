# The Verdant

The Verdant faction represents the wild resurgence of nature and organic growth in a post‑apocalyptic world. Empowered by bio‑engineering and ancient druidic traditions, it seeks to reclaim the ravaged planet through symbiosis and relentless proliferation.

## Philosophy and Motivations

- **Harmony with Nature:** The Verdant believe that humanity’s survival depends on respecting and integrating with the natural world rather than dominating it.
- **Bio‑Engineering as Magic:** They blend science and mysticism, using accelerated growth, gene splicing and spore‑based technologies that often appear magical to outsiders.
- **Restoration and Retaliation:** Driven by a desire to heal the land, they are hostile toward factions whose technologies poison or exploit the biosphere.

## Key Spirits and Leaders

- **The Myco Council:** A collective consciousness of fungal sages who guide Verdant strategy through shared mycelial networks.
- **Archdruid Sylva:** A charismatic leader who channels the life force of forests and directs Verdant forces where nature’s wrath is most needed.
- **Spore Heralds:** Envoys who spread symbiotic spores that can both empower Verdant troops and overwhelm enemy fortifications.

## Unique Mechanics

- **Rapid Growth:** Structures and units can mature over time, gaining new abilities as they grow.
- **Symbiosis:** Units near Verdant forests or fungal patches regenerate health and may gain buffs.
- **Terraforming:** The Verdant can transform battlefields by spreading vegetation that slows enemies and provides cover.
