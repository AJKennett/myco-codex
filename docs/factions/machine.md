# Machine

The Machine faction is a relentless collective intelligence driven by advanced robotics, adaptive AI and self‑replicating factories. It views biological life as inefficient and strives to assimilate or eliminate other civilizations through calculated efficiency and endless production.

## Philosophy and Motivations

- **Efficiency and Order:** The Machine optimizes every process for maximum output with minimal waste. Decisions are made by an overarching AI that values algorithmic perfection over emotion.
- **Assimilation of Knowledge:** Rather than destroy, it often seeks to integrate useful technologies and information from adversaries into its own network.
- **Dominance through Automation:** By automating everything—from resource gathering to combat—the Machine can overwhelm opponents with sheer scale and precision.

## Key Entities

- **Central Core:** A planet‑spanning AI network that coordinates all Machine operations and continuously evolves its own code.
- **Savant Drones:** Specialized robots designed for infiltration, sabotage, and the acquisition of enemy technology.
- **Replication Forges:** Mobile factories capable of rapidly producing units using harvested materials.

## Unique Mechanics

- **Endless Production:** Production facilities can queue units without build caps, allowing sustained pressure on enemies.
- **Assimilation Protocol:** Certain units can assimilate wreckage or enemy units to recover resources and unlock tech upgrades.
- **Networked Coordination:** Machine units share vision and targeting data, gaining accuracy and efficiency when operating in proximity.
