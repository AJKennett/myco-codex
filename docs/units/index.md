# Units

This section provides an overview of the major unit types available to each faction. Detailed stats and advanced tactics will be added as the codex grows.

## The Last Bastion

- **Sentinel Infantry:** Sturdy soldiers equipped with modular weapons and adaptive armor; they form the backbone of Bastion forces.
- **Bastion Engineer:** Support units that repair structures and salvage resources; they can deploy turrets or mines to fortify positions.
- **Guardian Tank:** Heavily armored vehicles with rotating turrets; effective at holding choke points and repelling assaults.
- - **Tetherdrone Worker:** Remote-operated drones that gather resources, construct structures, and repair mechanical units; they function optimally within relay network coverage, slowing down when outside of linked networks.

## Machine

- **Drone Swarm:** Light, fast‑produced flying drones that scout and harass enemies in large numbers.
- **Assimilator Bot:** Medium units capable of breaking down wreckage or enemy corpses to reclaim resources and unlock tech upgrades.
- **Siege Walker:** Massive quadruped robots armed with long‑range cannons; slow but devastating against fortifications.

## The Verdant

- **Vinebound Warrior:** Agile melee fighters that entangle enemies with living vines, limiting movement and rendering foes vulnerable.
- **Sporecaster:** Ranged units that launch spores to heal allies or poison foes; can seed fungal patches on the battlefield.
- **Grove Behemoth:** Towering creatures grown from ancient seeds; slow but nearly unstoppable and can terraform terrain as they move.
