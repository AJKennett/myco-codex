    The Last Bastion

    The Last Bastion is humanity's final stronghold amidst a war‑torn world dominated by relentless machines and rampant bio‑mutation. Formed from the remnants of surviving nations, this faction prioritizes sturdy defenses, community resilience, and tactical innovation.

    Philosophy and Motivations

    • Survival and Unity: The Last Bastion's primary goal is to ensure the survival of human culture and knowledge. They foster unity among disparate survivor groups and maintain a strong sense of identity and tradition.
    • Adapted Technology: They adapt salvaged pre‑war technology and innovative engineering to craft defensive structures and versatile units.
    • Cross‑Faction Diplomacy: While wary of the Machine and the Verdant, they will form temporary alliances when survival demands it.

    Key Leaders

    • Commander Aria Stone: A seasoned tactician who organizes defensive strategies and coordinates with allied factions.
    • Engineer Malik Chen: Responsible for adapting and inventing technologies that keep the Bastion ahead of the relentless assault.

    Unique Mechanics

    • Defensive Fortifications: The faction excels at building walls, turrets, and shield generators that can be upgraded over time.
    • Morale System: Units gain bonuses when fighting near fortifications and leaders, reflecting their resolve to protect their home.
    • Salvage and Retrofit: Many units can be improved by salvaging materials from the battlefield or from old world ruins, allowing for flexible tech progression.
